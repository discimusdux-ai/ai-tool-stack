## Key Features

### 1. AI clipping with a Virality Score

Upload a video or paste a link from YouTube, Zoom, Riverside, StreamYard, Twitch, Dropbox and others. OpusClip returns a set of short clips, each with a Virality Score: OpusClip's own estimate of how well the clip's hook, flow and topic will perform. Use it to rank clips, not to predict views.

### 2. ClipAnything (prompt-based clipping)

Earlier clippers only really worked on talking heads. **ClipAnything** analyzes visuals, audio and sentiment frame by frame, so it also works on vlogs, sports, gameplay and footage with little dialogue. You can prompt it in plain English ("every time someone laughs," "the part about pricing") to pull specific moments.

### 3. ReframeAnything and captions

Auto-reframe tracks the subject and converts clips to 9:16, 1:1 or 16:9. Animated captions come in 20+ languages, with emoji and keyword highlighting. Paid plans add filler-word and silence removal, and Pro adds speech enhancement and AI B-roll.

### 4. Publishing and scheduling

Paid plans can post directly to YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels. Pro adds a scheduler, six social account connections and more destinations (X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Pinterest, Threads and Bluesky).

### 5. Editor exports, API and an MCP server

Pro exports to **Adobe Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve**, so you can use OpusClip to find clips and do the final edit in a real editor. There's also limited API access. OpusClip now offers an **MCP server**, so Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor can clip, caption, reframe and schedule videos as tools.

### The meter: you pay per minute uploaded, not per clip kept

OpusClip's help center is clear about this: **1 credit = 1 minute of the original video you import**. Partial minutes round down, and anything under a minute costs 1 credit. So:

- A 60-minute podcast costs **60 credits**, whether you keep 2 clips or 20.
- Pro's 300 credits cover about **five hour-long episodes a month**.
- Free's 60 credits cover one hour-long video, and the clips must be exported within 3 days.

Trim intros, ads and dead air before you upload. Every minute you cut is a credit saved.

## Where OpusClip Breaks

Reviews are split. The iOS/Mac app averages about **4.9 from roughly 7,000 ratings**. Trustpilot sits around **4.0 from about 500 reviews**, with about 20% one-star (figures as reported by an independent review on September 25, 2026). The recurring complaints:

1. **Processing failures.** Projects hang for hours or never finish, especially during busy periods. Some long-time users say reliability has got worse.
2. **Billing disputes.** Users report charges after cancelling, credits expiring and refunds that are hard to get. OpusClip doesn't refund subscription fees.
3. **Clips that miss the point.** Some clips end too early or skip the best moment. The AI is good at finding moments, but not at telling a story.
4. **Caption typos.** Names, brands and jargon often need fixing by hand.
5. **It's not a full editor.** You can trim and style, but for real editing you'll export to Premiere or Resolve (Pro), or pair it with Descript.

## OpusClip FAQ

**Is OpusClip free?**
Yes. The free plan gives 60 credits (60 minutes of uploaded video) a month, with watermarked clips that must be exported within 3 days. It's good for testing, not for a publishing workflow.

**How are OpusClip credits counted?**
One credit per minute of the original video, rounded down for partial minutes (videos under a minute cost 1 credit). The number of clips you export doesn't change the cost.

**Starter or Pro?**
If you'll use it for a year, annual Pro ($14.50/mo) beats Starter ($15/mo) on credits, seats and features. Starter is only better if you want to stay month-to-month on a small budget.

**Is the Virality Score accurate?**
It's OpusClip's own estimate based on hook, flow and topic. Treat it as a way to rank clips for review, not a prediction of views.

**Can OpusClip post for me?**
Yes. Paid plans auto-post to YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels, and Pro adds a scheduler and more networks. Always check captions for typos in names and brand terms first.
