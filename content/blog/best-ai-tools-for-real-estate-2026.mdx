Every real estate tech vendor in 2026 calls itself "AI-powered." Most of them are selling one of two very different things, and agents who don't separate them end up paying a seat license for a tool they use four times a year, or a per-listing fee for something that should run all day in the background.

There is no single best AI tool for real estate. There is a best tool for your **leads** and a best tool for your **listings**, and they are priced in completely different ways. This roundup sorts seven tools by that line, quotes the vendor's own pricing wherever it exists, and names the places each one breaks.

## How we judged them: does it touch the lead, or the listing?

This is the one question that makes the rest of the buying decision easy.

**Lead-side tools** (CRMs, AI inside-sales agents) sit on your database every single day. They are priced per seat or per conversation, they get more valuable the longer you use them, and switching away is painful because your history, action plans and texting threads live inside them. Here you are buying a long relationship, so judge the vendor's pricing behaviour and support as hard as the features.

**Listing-side tools** (staging, photo editing, descriptions, 3D tours) are used in bursts, around each new listing. Real estate is seasonal. A tool you use for 40 listings in May and 6 in December should be billed monthly, cancelled in quiet months, and never prepaid annually unless the discount is large. Here you are buying output, so judge cost per listing and quality on the first try.

The second filter is compliance, and it now applies to the listing side in a concrete way. California's **AB 723** (chaptered in 2025) added Business and Professions Code section 10140.8: an agent who uses a digitally altered image in an ad, including one altered "through the use of photo editing software or artificial intelligence to add, remove, or change elements," must put a conspicuous disclosure next to the image and link to the original, unaltered photo. Even if you're outside California, expect MLS rules to move in the same direction. Every AI staging and photo tool below produces exactly the kind of image this law is about. On the description side, any AI copy still has to pass Fair Housing review. "Perfect for young families" was a problem before AI and it's still a problem when a model writes it.

Pricing below comes from each vendor's own pricing page or help center, checked October 2026, unless we say otherwise. Where a vendor hides its prices, we say so and label third-party figures as reported.

## 1. Follow Up Boss — best CRM for most agents

**Best for:** solo agents through mid-size teams who want lead routing, texting and action plans without a sales call.
**Price:** Grow $69/user/month ($58 billed annually). Pro $499/month including 10 users, extra users $49 ($416/month annual, extra users $41). Platform $1,000/month including 30 users, extra users $20 ($833 annual, extra users $17). Calling is an add-on: $39/user monthly, $33 annual. No contract, free trial.

Follow Up Boss is the CRM most agents end up on after trying an all-in-one, and the reason is boring: it does lead intake from every portal, routes leads fast, and keeps texts, calls and email in one timeline. The AI here is useful rather than flashy. It helps with summaries and drafting, but the real value is the plumbing: hundreds of lead-source integrations and Smart Lists that tell you who to call today.

It's also one of the few vendors in this category that publishes its prices, which makes the team math easy. BrokerDIY's breakdown (September 2026) found the crossover from Grow to Pro sits at **8 seats**: eight agents on Grow cost $552/month, already more than Pro's $499 for ten. A 25-person team pays $1,234/month on Pro and $1,000 on Platform, so Platform wins there too.

**Where it breaks**
- **Per-seat pricing hurts as you add support staff.** A long-time customer's open letter to the executive team (July 2026) said he was told Follow Up Boss "does not price match, does not offer unlimited users," and he was welcome to cancel. GoHighLevel-style unlimited-user platforms are the explicit comparison.
- **Calling costs extra** on Grow, so the real solo price with a dialer is $108/month, not $69.
- **No website or IDX.** You'll pay for a site separately, which is exactly the gap Lofty sells into.
- **No lead generation.** It handles leads you already get; it doesn't buy them for you.

## 2. Lofty — best all-in-one if you'll actually use all of it

**Best for:** teams and brokerages that want CRM, IDX website, marketing automation and paid lead gen under one login.
**Price:** quote only. Lofty's pricing page is a "Request Pricing" form. Third-party sources (Luxury Presence, May 2026; TechRadar) report a Core plan around **$449/month** with setup fees reported from $299 to $1,499. Lofty's own help center documents a **20% ad management fee** on most lead-gen ad products, a $150/month flat fee for budgets under $750, and 15% on certain branding ads.

Lofty (formerly Chime) is the "one bill" pitch done well on paper: an AI-powered CRM, a customisable IDX site, unlimited basic CMAs, a dialer, texting, and an AI assistant that works new leads. If you'd otherwise pay for Follow Up Boss plus a site builder plus a lead-gen agency, the bundle can come out ahead.

**Where it breaks**
- **You can't price it without a sales call.** Hidden pricing plus a reported setup fee means the first-year cost is unknowable until you're on the phone.
- **The ad fee is a real tax.** 20% on top of your ad spend means a $2,000/month lead-gen budget costs $2,400.
- **Support is the most common complaint.** TechRadar's review called customer service "seriously lacking," citing slow responses and scripted answers, and Reddit threads describe support in much harsher terms.
- **All-in-one means all-in.** Leaving later means rebuilding your site, your CRM and your automations at the same time.

## 3. Structurely — best AI inside-sales agent

**Best for:** teams buying online leads at volume who can't get a human on the phone within five minutes.
**Price:** platform fee (not published) plus token-based usage. Vendor-listed usage starts at **$0.07/minute for AI voice, $0.02/SMS, $0.02/AI email**. No per-seat licensing; plans are Small Team, Large Team and Enterprise.

Structurely's AI (the assistant has long been branded Aisa Holmes) texts, emails and now calls new leads, qualifies them over days or weeks, and hands warm ones to a human with live transfers or booked appointments. It doesn't generate leads. It works the ones you already pay Zillow, Realtor.com or Facebook for, which is precisely where most teams leak money: leads that never get a second follow-up.

The usage model is honest. You pay for conversations that happen, not for idle seats. That also means your bill grows with your lead flow, which is what you want, as long as you watch it.

**Where it breaks**
- **The platform fee isn't public,** so the "as low as" per-minute prices don't tell you your real monthly floor.
- **A meter is not a price.** A busy month of AI calling can cost far more than a quiet one. Set alerts.
- **AI on the phone carries consent risk.** Automated calls and texts to leads fall under TCPA consent rules. Make sure your lead sources' opt-in language actually covers AI outreach before you switch on voice.
- **It needs clean CRM integration.** On the Small Team plan, CRM integrations are listed as "Basic." Bad handoffs wipe out the benefit.

## 4. Virtual Staging AI — best cheap virtual staging

**Best for:** agents staging vacant rooms on a handful of listings a month.
**Price:** Basic $16/month (6 photos), Standard $19 (20 photos), Professional $39 (60), Enterprise $79 (150). Unlimited re-renders per photo. Annual billing is 12x monthly, no discount.

Upload an empty room, pick a style, and get a staged version in seconds. The pricing detail that matters is **unlimited renders per photo**. In practice one usable result often takes three to five generations, and here the retries don't count against your quota. That makes the per-photo math honest: about $0.95 per photo on Standard against the $16–$23 per image human-edited services charge, or the $2,000+ per room physical staging runs.

**Where it breaks**
- **No unlimited tier.** 150 photos for $79 is the ceiling, which rules it out for photography businesses.
- **No annual discount,** so there's no reason to prepay. Stay monthly and cancel in slow months.
- **Furniture scale and perspective still go wrong** on odd angles and small rooms. Expect retries.
- **Disclosure is on you.** Under AB 723 in California, every staged photo needs a disclosure next to it and a link to the original. The tool won't do that for you.

## 5. Fotello — best for photographers and high-volume listing media

**Best for:** real estate photographers and teams producing whole listing galleries each week.
**Price:** per listing, not per photo. Vendor tiers run from pay-as-you-go to plans around **$16 per listing** (up to 50 photos, unlimited human revisions) and **$18 per listing** (up to 75 photos) on a 75-listings-a-month plan, with AI twilight and staging creations included per listing. Booking and delivery are free.

Fotello combines AI editing (sky replacement, twilights, staging, cleanup) with human editors who fix what the AI gets wrong. Per-listing pricing is the right model for a media business: you know your cost per job before you shoot it. Fotello's own calculator puts 75 listings at $1,350/month against $4,125 for a typical per-photo editing setup. That's a vendor figure, so run your own numbers, but the shape is right.

**Where it breaks**
- **Built for media businesses, not individual agents.** A solo agent with three listings a month is better off with a simple staging tool.
- **Volume tiers commit you to a monthly listing count.** Seasonal businesses should check how unused listings are handled before choosing a tier.
- **Revisions are capped by type:** human revisions are unlimited, but the AI revisions are counted per listing (2 or 4 depending on tier).
- **Same disclosure problem as staging.** A replaced sky or a virtual twilight is an altered image under AB 723's definition.

## 6. ListingAI — best cheap toolkit for descriptions and listing marketing

**Best for:** agents who want listing descriptions, single-property sites, social posts and some staging for under $40/month.
**Price:** Free plan; Essential $19/month ($17 annual); Professional $36/month ($32.50 annual, includes 300 image credits/month for AI staging and edits); a $150/month top tier. Note: the dated snapshot of this pricing page we could find is from late 2024. Confirm at checkout.

ListingAI is the Swiss-army option: write the MLS description, spin up a listing website, generate social captions and presentation videos, and run a few staging edits, all from one listing record. For a solo agent, it replaces three or four small subscriptions.

**Where it breaks**
- **Jack of all trades.** The staging is fine for occasional use. A dedicated tool like Virtual Staging AI is better for heavy staging.
- **Generic copy without editing.** Any AI description reads like every other AI description unless you add the specifics only you know: the street noise, the light in the kitchen at 4pm.
- **Fair Housing review is still your job.** "Fair-housing-safe" claims don't move liability off the licensee.
- **Pricing visibility.** We couldn't confirm a fresh 2026 snapshot of the pricing page, so treat these numbers as a starting point.

## 7. Matterport — best 3D tours, with a subscription you have to watch

**Best for:** listings where remote or out-of-state buyers matter, plus luxury and new construction.
**Price:** Matterport's own price list (updated July 29, 2026) includes a Free plan, a **Starter 5 plan at $14/month** (5 active spaces, phone capture only), and Professional plans starting at **$69/month ($696/year)**, scaling by number of active spaces up to Professional 125 at $365/month.

Matterport is still the default name in 3D tours, and phone capture has brought the entry cost down a lot. The AI work happens in processing: building the 3D model, auto-measurements, floor plans and room labelling. For a listing with a lot of out-of-town interest, a tour can save a dozen wasted showings.

**Where it breaks**
- **Active-space limits are the real meter.** Every live tour counts against your tier. Archive old listings or you'll get pushed up a plan.
- **One-way doors.** Professional plans **cannot downgrade** to Starter or Free, and Pro3/Pro2 camera scans can't be uploaded to Starter plans.
- **Auto-renewal complaints.** App-store reviews describe renewals "without proper notice" and refused refunds after prompt cancellation.
- **Ownership shift.** CoStar now owns Matterport. Reviewers have flagged less visible innovation and possible tier changes. Plan for price moves.

## Sidebar: what we left out, and why

**kvCORE / BoldTrail.** kvCORE is being phased out and migrated to BoldTrail (per BoldTrail's own April 2026 post). It's a capable platform that brokerages usually provide to their agents. Pricing is quote-based and add-ons like BackOffice cost extra. If your brokerage gives it to you for free, use it before buying Lofty.

**Write.Homes.** A solid listing-description alternative to ListingAI: Agent Essentials $25/month (500 AI credits), Team Accelerator $250, Brokerage $1,500, 14-day trial without a card. It's credit-metered, while ListingAI is feature-tiered.

**Plain ChatGPT or Claude.** For descriptions alone, a $20/month general assistant with a good prompt and your own notes beats most real-estate-branded writers. You lose MLS integration and templates. You gain a tool that also does your emails, CMA commentary and contract summaries (summaries, not legal advice).

## What we'd actually buy

**Solo agent, 10–15 deals a year (~$1,000/year):** Follow Up Boss Grow annual ($696) + Virtual Staging AI Standard only in listing months (~6 months × $19 = $114) + ListingAI Essential annual ($204). Skip Matterport unless a listing calls for it.

**Five-agent team (~$4,800/year plus usage):** Follow Up Boss Grow annual for five ($3,480) + calling for two ISAs ($792) + ListingAI Professional annual ($390) + Matterport Starter 5 ($168). Add Structurely once lead volume clearly beats your team's callback speed, and budget the usage line separately.

**Brokerage or 25+ agents:** Follow Up Boss Platform ($10,000/year annual for 30 users) or BoldTrail if your franchise provides it. Lofty only if you'll use the site and lead gen, and only after you've seen the setup fee and the ad-fee math in writing.

## Bottom line

Put your money on the lead side first. A CRM that gets every lead a fast, consistent follow-up pays for itself before any staging tool does. On the listing side, buy monthly, cancel when it's quiet, and treat disclosure as part of the workflow, not an afterthought: if AI changed the photo, say so and link the original.

Our default stack: **Follow Up Boss** for leads, **Virtual Staging AI** for staging, and either **ListingAI** or a general assistant for copy. Everything else is for a specific problem you can name.
