# Fliki Review 2026: Text-to-Video That's Cheap Until You Touch AI Footage

Fliki takes something you've already written, such as a script, a blog post or a PowerPoint deck, and turns it into a narrated video. It splits the text into scenes, writes voiceover-ready narration, matches stock visuals, adds captions and music, and gives you a draft in a few minutes.

For faceless YouTube channels, explainer videos, course content and repurposing articles into social video, it's one of the most efficient tools available in 2026. A stock-footage video with a standard voice costs almost nothing. A few seconds of AI-generated footage can use up a month of credits.

## What Fliki Does Well

### Script, blog or slides to video in minutes

Paste a script, an idea or a blog URL, or upload a PPT. Fliki splits the text into scenes at natural sentence breaks, picks visuals for each scene, generates the voiceover and adds captions and background music. You then edit scene by scene: swap a voice, change a clip, rewrite a line and regenerate only that scene.

### The voice library

Fliki's real advantage is audio. It lists **2,000+ AI voices in 80+ languages and 100+ accents**, and you can adjust pace, pitch and pauses for each scene. Paid plans add voice cloning (upload a short sample and reuse your own voice), and Fliki says its cloning works across 30+ languages. If you run a voiceover-led channel in more than one language, very few tools match this.

### Translation and dubbing

You can translate a finished video into 80+ languages with a native voiceover and translated captions, and the higher tiers add lip-synced dubbing. That's a cheap way to test a second-language channel without hiring voice actors.

### Avatars and AI clips (with caveats)

Standard includes voice cloning and a limited set of stock avatars. Premium unlocks all avatars, photo avatars and AI-generated video clips. They work, but Fliki is a voiceover-and-visuals tool first. If you want a realistic talking presenter, Synthesia and HeyGen are built for that ([see our video tools guide](/blog/best-ai-video-tools-2026-deep)).

### The meter is the price, not the sticker

Fliki's credit guide sets out what each part of a video costs:

- **Voiceover:** 0.25, 0.5, 1 or 2 credits per minute, depending on the voice's color band in the picker (blue, green, yellow or purple)
- **Stock or uploaded media:** 0 credits
- **Uploaded audio:** 0 credits
- **AI images:** 0.05–1.5 credits per image
- **AI video:** **0.1–6 credits per second**, depending on model and resolution

Here's what that means in practice. Standard gives you 2,160 credits a year, about 180 a month.

- A 5-minute stock-footage video with a green voice costs **2.5 credits**. That's roughly 70 of those videos a month.
- The same 180 credits buy as little as **30 seconds** of AI-generated footage on the most expensive model.

So Fliki is excellent value for voiceover-on-stock videos and expensive for AI-generated visuals. Choose your plan based on which of those you make. One more detail: some voices run on a lighter engine on the Free plan and a better one on paid plans, so the same voice can sit in a different price band depending on your plan. The picker always shows the rate you'll pay.

## Where Fliki Breaks

Fliki has around 3,000 Trustpilot reviews, and the complaints there are consistent. You should know them before you buy:

1. **Cancelling and refunds.** Several reviewers say they couldn't find a cancel option, or were refused refunds after a short time on an annual plan. If you're unsure, **start monthly**.
2. **Credits lost to failed generations.** Users report voices skipping words, which means regenerating scenes and spending credits again, and Fliki refusing to restore those credits.
3. **"AI video" that barely moves.** Some buyers expected generative footage and got pans and zooms over stills. Premium's AI clips are real, but most videos are mostly stock footage.
4. **YouTube copyright flags.** Some users say videos built from stock assets were flagged or demonetized. Fliki says its assets are royalty-free, but stock libraries aren't exclusive, and Content ID matches can still happen.
5. **Slow support and outdated docs.** This is the most common non-billing complaint.

To be fair, plenty of the same reviewers say video creation is "a breeze," and the free plan is generous enough to learn the tool before paying.

## Who Fliki Is For

- **Faceless YouTube and TikTok channels** built on narration plus visuals

## Who Should Skip It

- Anyone who mainly wants **cinematic AI-generated footage**. Use Runway, Kling or Google's Veo, and budget per second.
- Teams who need a **realistic on-camera presenter**. Synthesia and HeyGen do this better.
- Creators editing **footage they've already shot**. Opus Clip (for clips) or Descript (for editing) fit better ([our Opus Clip review](/blog/opus-clip-review-2026)).

## Getting Started

3. Avoid AI-footage scenes until you've seen how many credits a few seconds costs.
4. Upgrade to Standard **monthly** first. Switch to annual only after a month of steady use.

## Fliki FAQ

**Is Fliki free?**
Yes. The free plan gives 3 credits a month, 300 voices, 720p export and a Fliki watermark. That's enough for a few short videos with standard voices. Commercial rights and 1080p need Standard.

**How many videos does Fliki Standard make per month?**
It depends on the voice tier and visuals. With stock footage and a mid-tier (green) voice, a 5-minute video costs about 2.5 credits, so Standard's ~180 monthly credits cover dozens of videos. Add AI-generated clips and that number drops fast, because AI video is charged per second.

**Can I use Fliki videos on monetized YouTube channels?**
Paid plans include commercial rights. Some users report Content ID flags on stock-heavy videos, because stock libraries aren't exclusive. Use your own footage or AI images for key scenes if monetization matters.

**Can I clone my own voice?**
Yes, on Standard and above. Premium allows multiple voice clones. Fliki says cloning works across 30+ languages, so you can dub videos in your own voice.

**Monthly or annual?**
Monthly first. Annual saves 25%, but refund complaints on annual plans are the most common issue in reviews.

## Bottom Line

Fliki is the best-value tool in 2026 for turning written content into narrated video, mainly because of its voice library. At $21–28 a month, voiceover-on-stock videos cost very little per video. AI footage is where credits disappear, and support and cancellation are the areas to be careful with. Start on the free plan, stay monthly at first, and Fliki can replace a voice actor and an editor for most explainer-style content.
